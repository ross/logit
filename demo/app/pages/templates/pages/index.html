Hello from the logit demo

This page is served by a real Django app behind haproxy and nginx -- every request through that chain gets a W3C traceparent minted at the edge, and all four tiers (haproxy, nginx, this app, and logit itself) agree on the same trace id: this app's own request span is a real OpenTelemetry span, exported over OTLP straight to Tempo (deliberately bypassing logit -- not every telemetry leg needs it), alongside its structured access log over syslog through logit as usual.

Generate some traffic by hand

traffic (demo/compose.yaml) already drives a low-volume loop through these, but you can trigger them directly too: /work (jittered latency, an occasional real 503) and /boom (always a real 500, with a real exception recorded on its OpenTelemetry span) -- both give the demo's Tempo traces a shape worth looking at, instead of a flat chain of 200s.

Unlike the plain link above, this goes through @opentelemetry/instrumentation-fetch (browser/telemetry.js) -- the resulting fetch span becomes a real parent of this call's own Django request span, one trace spanning browser → haproxy → nginx → app → Postgres/Celery.
