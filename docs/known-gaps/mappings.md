# Known gaps: Cross-protocol mappings

Entry format and the other areas: [the known-gaps index](README.md).

- **Cross-protocol semantic gaps.** Each row below is a place where `logit`'s internal model and a
  peer wire model can't express each other cleanly. Each mapping is counted and documented at its
  own call site; this entry collects them in one place. Tracked as debt against
  [ADR `lossless-transit`](../adr/lossless-transit.md), whose "Residual debt" section lists the
  like-to-like residuals. Each codec's module doc is
  the authority for its rows, except where `spec/transit/mapping.yaml` has a column for the sink:
  the generated tables after the hand-written one are that column's gaps, and the mapping table
  is their authority:
  - **Unlabeled `encode`/`decode` rows**, and `encode (OTLP)` in the generated tables, are OTLP
    (`crates/logit-proto/src/otlp/`).
  - **`encode (Prometheus)`/`decode (Prometheus)`** is the exposition/OpenMetrics and
    remote-write codec (`crates/logit-proto/src/prometheus/`,
    [ADR `prometheus-scrape-and-exposition`](../adr/prometheus-scrape-and-exposition.md)). Its
    *decode* direction is close enough to lossless that most rows are on the way out. Apart from
    the remote-write reserved-prefix row, the `decode (Prometheus)` rows are the exposition shapes
    Prometheus's own parser reads differently, each pinned by a case in the Prometheus text
    differential corpus (`testdata/differential/prometheus-text/`); no recorded exporter's body
    reaches one.
  - **`encode (collectd)`/`decode (collectd)`** is the collectd binary-protocol codec
    (`crates/logit-proto/src/collectd/`,
    [ADR `collectd-binary-relay`](../adr/collectd-binary-relay.md)).
    collectd has *fewer* numeric kinds than the model, so its rows are mostly "no wire form
    exists" rather than "the nearest shape loses something."
  - **`encode (Graphite)`/`decode (Graphite)`** is the Graphite/Carbon codec (`crates/logit-proto/src/graphite/`,
    [ADR `graphite-carbon-relay`](../adr/graphite-carbon-relay.md)), the narrowest wire model: a
    carbon datapoint is one untyped number at one whole second.
  - **`encode (Datadog)`/`decode (Datadog)`** is the Datadog codec
    (`crates/logit-proto/src/datadog/`,
    [ADR `datadog-agent-and-intake-relay`](../adr/datadog-agent-and-intake-relay.md)); its module
    doc's tables are the authority. The `encode` rows cover what leaves `datadog_out` and
    `datadog_trace_out` from another protocol's data; a Datadog-origin batch relays losslessly.
  - **`encode (statsd)`/`decode (statsd)`** is the statsd codec (`crates/logit-proto/src/statsd/`
    and `crates/logit-outputs/src/statsd.rs`); `docs/known-gaps/statsd.md` has its other gaps.
  - **`encode (Splunk)`/`decode (Splunk)`** is the Splunk HEC codec
    (`crates/logit-proto/src/splunk/`, [ADR `splunk-hec-relay`](../adr/splunk-hec-relay.md)), with
    its `logs`, `metrics`, and `spans` submodules. The rows cover what another protocol's data
    loses through `splunk_hec_out` and what comes back different through `splunk_hec_in`; a
    HEC-origin batch relays losslessly modulo the codec's permitted normalizations.
  - **`encode (InfluxDB)`/`decode (InfluxDB)`** is the InfluxDB line-protocol codec
    (`crates/logit-proto/src/influxdb/`, its module doc's "Encode" and "Decode" sections,
    [ADR `influxdb-line-protocol-relay`](../adr/influxdb-line-protocol-relay.md)). The `encode`
    rows cover what another protocol's data loses through `influxdb_out`, and what reaches other
    sinks from `influxdb_in`: the private `_logit.influxdb.*` carriers, string fields among them,
    never render there. The `decode` rows cover what `influxdb_in` refuses
    or drops that `influxd` accepts, or the reverse; the module doc's "Deviations from influxd"
    is the full list. The `line_protocol` transform decodes through the same code, but skips a
    bad line instead of failing the message and counts under `logit.transform.`; its module doc
    (`crates/logit-transforms/src/line_protocol.rs`) has the differences. `docs/design/internal-telemetry.md`'s `influxdb_out` section lists every counter.

  | encode | `MetricKind::Summary` with a quantile outside `[0, 1]` → OTLP `ValueAtQuantile` with that quantile unchanged | none (documented) | The proto's `ValueAtQuantile.quantile` comment says "Must be in the interval [0.0, 1.0]", and nothing on the way out enforces it. Materializing explicit buckets would be the lossy conversion `MetricKind::ExponentialHistogram` exists to avoid. |

The dropped kinds have no honest string form: a `Bytes` need not be UTF-8, and flattening a `Map` would invent unparseable syntax. |

  | encode (Prometheus) | `EventBatch::scope`, `Resource::schema_url`, and every `dropped_attributes_count` → dropped | none (documented) | Exposition has no scope, schema, or dropped-count concept: a family is a name, a type, two metadata strings, and labelled samples. `otlp_in -> prometheus_out` loses instrumentation-scope identity; `otlp_in -> otlp_out` doesn't.   | decode (Prometheus) | A sample value or timestamp the model can't hold: a NaN, infinite, or negative bucket, `_count`, or `_gcount`, or a timestamp past April 2262 → that line **dropped**; an OpenMetrics `info` sample other than 1, or a `stateset` sample other than 0 or 1 → read as 1 | `logit.input.metrics.dropped{reason="malformed_line"}` / none | A count is a `u64` (`crates/logit-proto/src/prometheus/assemble.rs`'s `count_value`) and an instant an `i64` of nanoseconds, which ends in 2262; Prometheus holds counts as floats and instants as milliseconds.   **Too narrow for a row:** `BodyFormat` has no OTLP field and round-trips through a reserved
  attribute (`logit.body_format`), lossless but attribute-shaped (`otlp/logs.rs`'s module doc).
  [ADR `lossless-transit`](../adr/lossless-transit.md) rule (c) names it the standing example of a
  `logit`-only concept with nowhere else on the wire to go, so it stays.

  **Qualification of an ADR:** `Distribution`→`Summary` and `Set`→skip narrow
  [ADR `native-wire-format-with-otlp-bridge`](../adr/native-wire-format-with-otlp-bridge.md)'s claim
  that the internal model "must be a superset of what OTLP can express, or the OTLP codec becomes
  lossy". Here `logit`'s own model (a mergeable sketch, a mergeable cardinality estimator) can't be
  losslessly re-expressed *as* OTLP, the direction that ADR didn't anticipate. See
  [ADR `committed-pregenerated-otlp-protobuf`](../adr/committed-pregenerated-otlp-protobuf.md)'s
  Consequences section, and `crates/logit-proto/src/otlp/metrics.rs`'s module doc for the full
  encode/decode tables this summarizes.

- **The class check can misread two `graphite_out` tag names that sanitize to one.** Where two
  attribute keys on a record, such as `a b` and `a_b`, render as the same tag name, the encoder
  keeps the one whose original name sorts first and counts the other
  `logit.output.tags.omitted{reason=collision}` (`build_tag_suffix` in
  `crates/logit-proto/src/graphite/encode.rs`). The class check's matcher builds the expected tags
  in a `BTreeMap` keyed by the rendered name (`Rec::public_as` in
  `crates/logit-cli/src/transit/classes.rs`), resource attributes first and then the event's, so
  it keeps whichever key it inserts last. When the two sides keep different keys with different
  values, the record pairs with nothing and the check fails, though the sink did what the codec
  documents. No case has such a collision today, and the counter has no row yet ("Counters no
  row names yet" in `spec/transit/README.md`). A case that adds one needs the matcher to keep
  the key the encoder keeps.
- **The class check can misread a resource attribute that an omitted event attribute
  shadows.** An event attribute whose value `graphite_out` writes no tag for (a timestamp,
  bytes, a map, null, an empty string, or an array with no representable element) can share its
  key with a resource attribute. The encoder merges the two with the event's value winning
  (`logit_core::attrs::merged`), then omits the tag, counted `logit.output.tags.omitted` with
  `reason=unrepresentable`, or `reason=empty` for an empty string, so neither value reaches the
  wire. The matcher inserts the resource's value and then skips the event's, so it expects the resource's
  value. The record still pairs, since the tags read back are a subset of the expected ones, but
  the `attributes/resource_attribute` field row reads `lost`, worse than its `normalized` cell,
  whose text permits the event's value winning on a shared key. The check fails, though the sink
  did what the cell says. No case reaches it today. A case that does needs the matcher to apply
  the event's omission to the shared key.
- **The class check can't pair `graphite_out` records that differ only by tags under
  `tags: drop`.** With no tag segment, such records read back as several datapoints on one path.
  A gauge pairs by value in the check's first pass, but a sum or a `multi_value: expand` series
  reads back as a gauge and pairs in the third pass (`classes.rs`'s "Pairing"). That pass gives
  an input every read-back record under its name when no other unpaired input shares both its
  name and its tags, and under `tags: drop` the inputs differ by tags, so the first input claims
  every read-back record and the rest read as unpaired, and the check fails. So the only flow
  under `tags: drop` is `graphite-relay-drop`, whose `graphite_in` cases hold only gauges, and
  the `tags: drop` cells of `bool_value`, `number_value`, `array_value`, `map_value`,
  `bytes_value`, `timestamp_value`, and `resource_attribute` are in `spec/transit/coverage.md`'s
  "Cells no flow reaches: graphite_out". Another source's flow under `tags: drop` needs pass 3 to
  share the read-back records of one path among the inputs that wrote them.
